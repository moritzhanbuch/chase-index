What an agent on both sides would change here
  • 47% of UK invoices are paid after the agreed terms. An agent on the AP side matches invoice, PO and proof of delivery and clears disputes with the supplier's agent before the due date, and the ERP posts only after human approval.
  • 3 UK legal entities report separately. Agents work the long tail of suppliers that AP teams rarely get to, across entities, and email suppliers that are not on the network yet.
  • Long payment days are leverage that can be priced. Buyer and supplier agents negotiate early payment against a discount, inside guardrails the CFO sets (minimum APR, maximum discount, blocklists).
