# Corrections

The official source wins. Every reported figure on this site links to the document it came from: the company's statutory report on GOV.UK, or its annual report filed in ESEF format. If a number here differs from that document, the document is right and this site is wrong.

## How to report an error

Write to `{{ contact }}` with:

1. The page or the company and the figure you think is wrong.
2. A link to the official source that shows the correct figure.
3. If the issue is that a UK legal entity is linked to the wrong group, the correct parent and how you know.

## What happens next

Requests are answered within 5 working days. Disputed figures are marked as under review while they are checked.

- Errors in parsing or matching are fixed in the pipeline and the whole site is rebuilt from the official data, so the fix applies everywhere.
- If a company has filed a corrected or newer report with the official service, the next data refresh picks it up automatically.
- If a company believes its own filed figure is wrong, the place to correct it is the official reporting service. This index mirrors what was filed.
- Material corrections are listed on this page with the date.

## A note on wording
