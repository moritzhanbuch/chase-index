Ask for the group treasurer or CFO office. Open with the public number: Puig's Spanish payment period is up 7 days to 59 days in FY2025 (legal maximum per payment 60). Since few invoices miss the legal limit, the long days are agreed terms: a working-capital topic. Ask whether they are a deliberate lever.
