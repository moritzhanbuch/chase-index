Suggested first conversation

Ask for the head of accounts payable or shared services. Open with the public number: GE Aerospace's UK entities pay in 55 days on average (weighted by invoice value, 3 entities; UK median 32). With 50% of invoices paid after the agreed terms, start with process: what stops an invoice from being approved on time (a missing PO match, an unconfirmed delivery, a supplier waiting on an answer)? The annual report discloses a supplier finance programme ($1.2bn outstanding at 2025-12): ask which suppliers it reaches, and did terms get longer when the programme came in?
