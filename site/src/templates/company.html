How to read this

Reported figures are what the company's own legal entities filed under a statutory duty. They cover the entities and the period shown, nothing more. A group can pay quickly in one country and slowly in another, and a long average can reflect long agreed terms as well as late payment.

The computed proxy answers a different question: how large trade payables are next to annual costs. Use it to compare groups roughly, not to say how many days a supplier waits.

Corrections

If a figure here differs from the official source, the official source wins. Each number links to it. See how to report an error.
