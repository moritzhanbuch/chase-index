The 60-day legal maximum

Spanish law on late payment (Ley 3/2004, as amended by Ley 15/2010) sets a default payment period of 30 days between companies, which the parties can extend by agreement to a maximum of 60 days. Ley 15/2010, as amended by Ley 18/2022, also requires companies to disclose their average supplier payment period, and since 2022 the volume and number of invoices paid within the legal maximum.

The PMP is a weighted average: the ratio of paid operations weighted by payments made, and the ratio of pending operations weighted by payments still outstanding. A PMP under 60 days does not mean that every invoice was paid within 60 days, and a PMP above 60 days is an average, not a finding about any single invoice.
