Suggested first conversation

Ask for the group treasurer or CFO office. Open with the public number: Mondelez International's UK entities pay in 86 days on average (weighted by invoice value, 5 entities; UK median 32), and Mondelez UK Confectionery Production Limited is up 9 days to 113 days year on year. Since only 5% of invoices are paid late, the long days are agreed terms: a working-capital topic. Ask whether they are a deliberate lever. The annual report discloses a supplier finance programme ($3.6bn outstanding at 2025-12): ask which suppliers it reaches, and did terms get longer when the programme came in?
