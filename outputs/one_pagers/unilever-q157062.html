What an agent on both sides would change here
  • The largest UK entity by invoice value paid 67% of invoices after 60 days and 3% late, so suppliers wait by contract, and agent-negotiated early-payment discounts put a price on that wait, above the CFO's minimum APR.

Suggested first conversation

Ask for the group treasurer or CFO office. Open with the public number: Unilever's UK entities pay in 69 days on average (weighted by invoice value, 3 entities; UK median 32), and Unilever U.K. Central Resources Limited is up 5 days to 74 days year on year. Since only 4% of invoices are paid late, the long days are agreed terms: a working-capital topic. Ask whether they are a deliberate lever. UK entities report offering supply chain finance: ask which suppliers it reaches, and did terms get longer when the programme came in?
