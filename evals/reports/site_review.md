# Site review: journalist and media lawyer

Reviewer: `critic` subagent (did not build the site). Read-only review.
Build reviewed: `site/dist` built 2026-11-16 15:41 (881 pages, 871 company pages), checked against `data/processed/*.parquet` as of 12:25 the same day. An earlier build (12:23) was out of sync with the data; all comparisons below were redone on the 15:41 build.
Network: three GOV.UK report pages fetched through `chase.common.http.fetch` to confirm source links. Nothing else.

## Verdicts

**Finance journalist: would cite the entity-level data and the headline numbers today. Would not cite any group-level figure, and would not link to the home table.**
The UK entity figures, the lookup, the Spain table and all five headline numbers reproduce exactly. The problem is the entity-to-group roll-up. A name-overlap scan of the published entity CSV found at least 35 UK entities attached to groups that do not own them (Aviva under BlackRock, Arriva under Deutsche Bahn, Marshalls plc under TJX, Aston Martin under Ford, SEFE under Gazprom). Almost all come from the two Wikidata methods and from treating a shareholder as a parent. One of those errors is enough for a journalist to stop trusting the group table. Second problem: the group average is value-weighted but labelled "mean" or "simple mean" in four places, and for Amazon the two differ by 60 days (98.4 vs 38.1).

**Media lawyer: not cleared for publication as built. Clearable once the four blockers are fixed.**
Wording is clean: no loaded terms, no individuals, no internal sales data, disclaimer and OGL line on every page, reported and computed figures separated. The exposure is factual accuracy, not tone. Stating that company X belongs to group Y and attributing X's payment figures to Y is a false statement of fact about both companies when the ownership is wrong. Conditions for clearance: (1) remove or fix the wrong group links (B1), (2) a real contact address and site URL (B2), (3) correct the description of the group average (B3), (4) stop presenting recently acquired, joint-venture and deconsolidated entities as plain group members (B4). The should-fix items S1 to S6 should be done before the link is sent to anyone outside the hiring process.
